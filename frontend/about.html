MY STORY

Started on Weekends. Never Stopped.

I started editing videos five years ago as a hobby — helping a friend cut down his YouTube uploads on weekends, just to see if I could make them better. I could.

Somewhere along the way I fell in love with the craft itself: storytelling through editing, sound design, and colour grading. What started as a favour for a friend turned into a full-time career.

What makes me different is that I don't just cut clips together — I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end.

My mission is simple: help creators and brands turn raw, unorganised footage into polished stories that actually grow their audience.
